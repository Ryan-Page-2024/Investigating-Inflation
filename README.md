# Investing-Inflation
I researched the 10 Year Breakeven Inflation Rate and Oil Price fluctuations, combining time series techniques such as Bollinger Bands, Exponential Smoothing, and Rolling Correlation to build descriptive and predictive models that help contextualize modern monetary policies that span from 2018 to 2026. Between 2018 and 2026, the Global economy was hit by a sequence of distinct structural shocks including the COVID-19 pandemic, aggressive tariffs, and war abroad. It’s my hope that this analysis is able to help you form more of an educated understanding on the history of recent economic events and decisions.
